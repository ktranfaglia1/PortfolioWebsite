## Overview

CarMart is an internal sales-operations tool for a fictional pre-owned car dealership.
Employees manage owners, vehicles, and sales through simple forms, without touching SQL. It
began as a database course's final project, and I later expanded it into a fuller app.

## What It Does

- **Sell:** record a sale in one form, covering the seller's contact details, the vehicle
  (make, model, year, mileage), and the registration (number, sale date, price).
- **Search:** find a vehicle or look up a seller, or list the full inventory with each
  vehicle's owner and sale details.
- **Modify:** update a vehicle's or owner's details, unlist a vehicle, or remove a seller.

## Database Design

A normalized MySQL schema with three tables: `Owner`, `Vehicle`, and `Registration`.
`Registration` links each sale to an owner and a vehicle through foreign keys, and the
inventory view joins all three tables to show each vehicle with its owner and sale.

## Limits

CarMart was built to show relational design and full create, read, update, and delete
operations in PHP and MySQL. It builds queries by string concatenation and has no
authentication; a production version would need parameterized queries, input validation,
and access control. The app is no longer hosted; the screenshots and source code show how it
worked.
