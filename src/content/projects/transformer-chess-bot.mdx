## Overview

Traditional engines like Stockfish play deterministic, superhuman chess. We wanted to know
whether techniques from language models could produce play that feels more human. The bot
pairs a transformer-style evaluator with Monte Carlo Tree Search, inspired by AlphaZero.

## My contributions

- Built the complete PySide6 GUI, including real-time evaluation visualization.
- Improved the core transformer model with self-attention.
- Cleaned and assembled training data from millions of Lichess games across skill levels, plus grandmaster games.

## Outcome

The model reached 92% prediction accuracy on grandmaster games. Its playing strength (an
estimated 400 Elo) fell short of our target, which taught us how much a strategic game AI
depends on the quality of its training data. The project still showed that language-model
techniques are a workable way to evaluate chess positions.
