## Overview

How much do player ratings and opening choices actually predict who wins a chess game? I
cleaned and analyzed more than six million games from Lichess.org to find out.

## Approach

- Cleaned and preprocessed the full dataset in Python.
- Mapped opening preferences across Elo groups and event types to see how strategy changes with skill.
- Built logistic regression and random forest models to test how well Elo and opening choice predict results.

## Findings

Elo difference was by far the strongest predictor. Adding opening features improved
accuracy only slightly, which points to the limits of Elo-based prediction and the need
for more contextual data.
