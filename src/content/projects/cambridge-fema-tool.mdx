## Overview

The City of Cambridge needed a clearer way to understand FEMA's numerical model predictions
for future flooding in Dorchester County. I built a Python GIS application that turns
those predictions into interactive, map-based views.

## Approach

- Combined LiDAR and DEM elevation data to model the terrain.
- Visualized flood and storm-surge projections for hurricanes and other storm events, with controls for water level, year, and hurricane category.
- Ran cost-benefit analyses on different prediction outcomes to estimate the financial and infrastructure impact of each scenario.

## Outcome

The tool gives local decision-makers a data-driven, easy-to-use way to plan for disaster
preparedness and mitigation.
