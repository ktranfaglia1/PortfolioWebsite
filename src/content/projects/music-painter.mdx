## Overview

Music Painter treats music as something you can look at. It reads a WAV file or a live
recording, extracts the dominant frequencies and spectral data, and paints the whole clip
as one 2-D image built from simple geometric shapes.

I built it from scratch with a mathematics student, under the guidance of a faculty mentor.

## Approach

- Read audio from WAV files (SciPy) or live input and recordings (PyAudio).
- Ran a Fast Fourier Transform over short chunks to get the dominant frequencies and spectral data for each slice of the clip.
- Designed 13 original algorithms that map that data to shapes, color, and position, so the same song produces a distinct image in each style.
- Wrapped it in a PySide2 desktop app with controls for algorithm, chunk size, and background color, plus render-while-playing, recording, image export, and printing.

## Outcome

The project was accepted at five conferences, including NCUR 2024, and the app is free to
download from [musicpainter.org](https://musicpainter.org).
