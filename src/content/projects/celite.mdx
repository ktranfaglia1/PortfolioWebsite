## Overview

Our client, a professor whose research focuses on discrete mathematical systems, wanted a
set of cellular automata simulators in the browser. Celite simulates Conway's Game of Life
and Wolfram rulesets, both sequential and parallel, without any framework.

## My role

I led a three-person team using Agile/Scrum:

- Ran team meetings and was the main contact with the client.
- Assigned roles, set weekly and quarterly agendas, prioritized tasks, and tracked progress.
- Owned the UI/UX and the entire front end as the sole front-end developer, and built parts of the back-end logic.

## Outcome

The client was very satisfied and used Celite in their research. The project was accepted
at SUSRC 2025 and is live at [celite.org](https://celite.org).
